Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026.

OutageReady is a small informational website. This page explains what information is collected when you visit.

What the calculator does with your inputs

The outage calculator runs entirely in your browser. The appliances and numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored on a server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It may set cookies and collect anonymized usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, or opt out of many third-party ad vendors at aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

Some links go to Amazon.com and include our Amazon Associates tracking ID. If you click one and buy, Amazon may set its own cookies and pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon’s use of data is governed by its own privacy notice. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Netlify, which may log visitor IP addresses as part of serving pages.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact page, we receive the name, email address and message you type, and use them only to reply.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data held about you. We do not keep personal data beyond contact messages, so requests about analytics or advertising data are best directed to Google through your Google account settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, and we do not run accounts or collect email addresses other than the ones people choose to send us through the contact form.
