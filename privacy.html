Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

OutageReady ("we") is a small informational website. This page explains what information is collected when you visit.

What the calculator does with your inputs

The outage calculator runs entirely in your browser. The appliances and numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored on a server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It may set cookies and collect anonymized usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site go to retailers such as Amazon. If you click one and buy, the retailer may set its own cookies to credit us. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, and we do not currently collect email addresses or run accounts.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data held about you. Because we do not keep personal data ourselves, requests about analytics data are best directed to Google through your Google account settings.
