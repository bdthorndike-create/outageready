Buying guides

Portable power stations: what size do you need?

A quick rule: phones, Wi-Fi and lights fit comfortably in a small unit (about 300-600 Wh). A modern fridge plus basics for a day wants 1,000-2,000 Wh. Whole-home needs, including heat pumps or electric water heaters, are where a permanently installed battery or generator is the honest answer.

Generator vs. battery: the real trade-offs

Generators run as long as you have fuel but need ventilation, storage and maintenance. Batteries are silent and indoor-safe but have a hard capacity limit. Many households do best with a battery for essentials and a generator as a long-outage backup.
