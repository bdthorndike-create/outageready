About OutageReady

An independent resource for sizing backup power at home.

What OutageReady does

OutageReady helps you work out how much backup power a home needs, then points you to the right class of equipment. The calculator adds up the watt-hours for the appliances you choose, multiplies by the length of the outage and adds a 15% buffer for inverter losses. The guides explain the same arithmetic in plain English.

How we pick

Size guidance comes from the arithmetic of what you want to run, not from which product pays more. We compare capacity, output, recharge options, warranty and price per watt-hour, and we say so when a smaller or cheaper option is the better fit. When a portable unit cannot do the job, we say that too.

How the site pays for itself

OutageReady is free to use. It earns money from advertising and from affiliate commissions when you buy through some links. The links on the calculator go to searches on Amazon for a size class, such as 1,000 Wh power stations, rather than endorsing one product. Read the full affiliate disclosure.

Your numbers stay with you

The calculator runs in your browser. The appliances and numbers you enter are not sent to us. Our analytics record that a calculation happened, plus the size class and total watt-hours it produced, but not which appliances you chose.

Safety and limits

Nothing here replaces advice from a licensed electrician. Connecting backup power to your home wiring, and running fuel-burning equipment, carry real safety risks. Never run a generator indoors or in an attached garage. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and local codes. Prices and availability change, so check the retailer’s page before you buy. Questions or corrections are welcome on the contact page.
