How long will a power station last? Runtime chart

How many hours common power stations run at typical loads, and how to work out your own runtime.

Updated November 1, 2026 · 3 min read

The formula

Runtime (hours) ≈ battery watt-hours × 0.85 ÷ device watts.

The 0.85 is an allowance for inverter and conversion losses. Real figures vary by model and by load, and very small loads can be less efficient than that.

Runtime chart

Battery50 W100 W200 W500 W
500 Wh8.5 h4.3 h2.1 h50 min
1,000 Wh17 h8.5 h4.3 h1.7 h
2,000 Wh34 h17 h8.5 h3.4 h

Example: a router (20 W) plus a laptop charger (60 W) plus two LED lamps (20 W) is about 100 W. A 1,000 Wh unit covers that for around 8 hours.

What breaks the chart

  • Cycling loads. Fridges and freezers run in bursts, so average draw is lower than their running watts.
  • Heaters and cooking. Anything that makes heat can pull 750-1,500 W, which turns hours into minutes.
  • Cold. Lithium batteries can lose usable capacity in the cold.
  • Capacity is not always usable. Check whether the rated Wh is total or usable.
