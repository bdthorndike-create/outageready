Can a portable power station run a refrigerator?

Usually yes, for a while. The two things to check are whether the unit can handle the fridge's startup surge, and whether it has enough stored energy to get you through the outage.

Updated November 1, 2026 · 5 min read

Step 1: surge watts

A refrigerator compressor draws extra power for a moment each time it kicks on. The power station's inverter needs both a rated (continuous) output that covers the running watts and a surge rating that covers the startup. Fridge labels list amps, and watts is roughly amps × volts (120 V in the US). Some fridges list a "locked rotor" or startup figure in the manual. If you can't find one, assume the startup is a few times higher than the running figure and leave headroom.

Step 2: daily energy use

A fridge does not run continuously; the compressor cycles. Look for the yellow EnergyGuide label, which gives annual kWh. Divide by 365 and multiply by 1,000 for daily Wh. For example, 400 kWh/year is about 1,100 Wh a day. A newer efficient fridge can be well below that and an old one well above it.

Fridge energy useApprox. Wh/dayWith ~15% losses
300 kWh/yr~820 Wh~945 Wh
400 kWh/yr~1,100 Wh~1,260 Wh
600 kWh/yr (older / large)~1,640 Wh~1,890 Wh

Compare that to the battery's capacity. A 1,000 Wh unit covers a typical modern fridge for less than a day. A 2,000 Wh unit covers it for a day or so, longer if the door stays closed and the kitchen is cool. Add the freezer if it is separate.

Making the battery last

  • Keep the doors shut. A closed fridge holds temperature for hours, and a closed full freezer holds for longer.
  • Run the fridge only part of the time, e.g. a few hours at a time, then unplug and let it coast. Use a thermometer to check it stays at safe temperatures (at or below 40°F for the fridge).
  • Move the most valuable items into the freezer or a cooler with ice if the outage extends.
  • Recharge with solar input or by running a generator outdoors.

Food safety

If perishable food has been above 40°F for more than about two hours, throw it out. When in doubt, don't risk it.
